How Cocktail Shaker Sort Works

  1. Compare adjacent values. If the left value is greater, swap them.
  2. Scan forward to fix the largest remaining value at the right end.
  3. Scan backward to fix the smallest remaining value at the left end.
  4. Repeat within the remaining range. Stop when a pass makes zero swaps or the range has at most one value.
